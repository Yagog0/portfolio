# Caricatura em pixel art

Gerada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (modo built-in), a partir de `imagens/145058720.jpeg`, a pedido do autor do portfólio.

Arquivo usado no site: `imagens/yago-pixel-caricatura.png`. O PNG original foi copiado sem alterações, preservando o canal alfa. A apresentação em preto e prata é aplicada com CSS.

## Prompt final

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: transparent hero portrait for Yago Maba's black-and-silver developer portfolio. Input image: edit target and identity reference, the supplied photograph of Yago. Transform this person into a polished pixel-art caricature, with an expressive slightly oversized head and a compact upper-body bust. Preserve his recognizable identity: short dark swept-up hair, strong dark eyebrows, brown eyes, thin moustache and small goatee, young adult face, white crewneck shirt. A relaxed confident expression with a very slight smile, head turned subtly three-quarter as in the photo, both arms down out of frame. Style: intentional hand-placed 16-bit pixel art, large crisp square pixel clusters, stepped outlines and strong readable silhouette, tasteful retro game character portrait, clear facial features, limited 12-to-16 color palette predominantly charcoal, slate, silver and off-white with restrained warm skin tones. No photorealistic texture, no halftone dots, no point cloud, no dithering noise, no glow. Transparent background with clean alpha. Centered head and shoulder bust, complete hair with generous margin above, torso ending near lower frame, character occupies about 80 percent of canvas height. No scenery, no text, no logos, no accessories. Actual transparency, no checkerboard painted into the image.
